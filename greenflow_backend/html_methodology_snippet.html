Where do the assumed 0.0002 / 0.0008 / 0.003 kWh come from?

Not invented. Epoch AI's 2025 measurements put LLM inference energy at 0.0001–0.002 Wh per output token (numerically the same range in kWh per 1,000 tokens). A Sept 2025 bottom-up study (H100 power draw ÷ measured throughput, PUE-adjusted) found a median of 0.34 Wh/query for frontier-scale models; OpenAI and Google disclose 0.34 Wh and 0.24 Wh per typical query. Small sits near the efficient end of the Epoch AI range, Medium tracks the OpenAI/Google disclosed averages, Large sits just above Epoch AI's upper bound. Price figures ($0.4 / $2 / $8 per million tokens) sit inside published 2025–2026 API pricing bands for budget/mid/premium models. The embodied-carbon figures (20/60/250 g CO2e per compute-hour) are the weakest of the three — an order-of-magnitude illustrative assumption about amortized hardware manufacturing emissions, not a benchmarked number.
